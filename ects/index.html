Rules & sources

120 ECTS total. The category minima sum to 102 ECTS. The remaining 18 may come from any category except seminar and thesis; they are not a separate free-elective requirement.

  • Major: 26 ECTS, including at least 16 core.
  • Minor: 18 ECTS. Interfocus: 16 ECTS from two distinct courses.
  • Course subtotal: 76 ECTS, including major, minor, interfocus, free electives and Science in Perspective.
  • Seminar: one course, 2 ECTS. Practical work: 8–10 ECTS.
  • Science in Perspective: 2–6 ECTS. Language-credit eligibility needs individual checking.
  • Master’s thesis: 30 ECTS. Thesis registration also requires the major, two interfocus courses, practical work and at most 8 missing non-thesis credits, plus admission requirements.

Credit totals cap Science in Perspective at 6, practical work at 10, seminar at 2 and thesis at 30; excess or conflicting assignments are flagged. Courses count once, in their assigned category. Failed courses do not count. Unscheduled plans do not count toward projections.

GPA

The current GPA weights each passed numeric grade by its ECTS. Pass/fail courses are excluded. The projected GPA adds enrolled and scheduled planned courses only when an expected grade is entered, including a planned thesis. Blank estimates, failed courses and unscheduled plans are excluded. Expected grades do not change earned grades or credits. The display rounds to two decimals; calculations use full precision.
